# New Apps

From DataMiner 10.6.0 [CU10]/10.7.0 [CU0]/10.7.1 onwards<!-- RN 45878 -->, you can add apps that are built outside DataMiner to your DataMiner System, in addition to the apps you create with [Low-Code Apps](xref:Application_framework). These apps are built for example with AI-assisted tooling such as the [DataMiner App Builder](xref:AIAgentMarketplace). For more information on how to get an app, see [Creating a new app](xref:Creating_a_new_app).

New Apps are a type of [DataMiner Apps](xref:DataMiner_Apps). Once an app has been uploaded, you open and manage it in the same way as any other DataMiner app. For a comparison with Low-Code Apps, see [Choosing between New Apps and Low-Code Apps](xref:Dashboards_and_Low_Code_Apps#choosing-between-new-apps-and-low-code-apps).

## Adding and updating a new app

You first [create a new app](xref:Creating_a_new_app), and then [upload it from the DataMiner landing page](xref:Uploading_a_new_app). New apps are first available as a draft version. When the app is ready, you can publish it.

New apps cannot be edited in DataMiner. To change an app, update it outside DataMiner and upload it again.

## Header bar buttons and menus

A new app can add its own buttons and menus to the standard [web app header bar](xref:Accessing_the_web_apps#web-app-header-bar). If you do not see the buttons or menus you expect, check with the developer of the app.
