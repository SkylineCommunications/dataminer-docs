# Needs attention

The *Needs Attention* page lists all SLAs that currently require your attention, for example because they are breached or degraded.

Use this page to quickly find and act on the SLAs that need attention, without having to scan the full [SLAs](xref:SLA_Manager_SLAs) inventory.
