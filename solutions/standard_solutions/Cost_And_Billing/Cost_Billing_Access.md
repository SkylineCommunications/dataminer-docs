# Accessing the Cost & Billing app

To access the Cost & Billing app:

1. Go to the [landing page](xref:Accessing_the_web_apps#dataminer-landing-page) of your DataMiner System.

1. Search for the Cost & Billing app and click the app tile to open it.

![The Cost & Billing app tile on the DataMiner System landing page](~/solutions/images/CostAndBilling_home_page_app.png)

Alternatively, you can look for the Cost & Billing app in the *Apps* pane of the DataMiner Cube sidebar, and open the app from there.
