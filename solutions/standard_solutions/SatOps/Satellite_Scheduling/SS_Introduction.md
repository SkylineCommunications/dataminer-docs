# Introduction

The Satellite Scheduling app lets you schedule the use of the transponder capacity modeled in the [Satellite Inventory](xref:Satellite_Inventory) app.

At the center of the app is a **transponder timeline** that shows all transponder slots and their bookings. Color coding makes it easy to see which jobs book which capacity of a transponder. The timeline automatically respects the transponder plan that is active at a given time, so only the slots that are actually available are offered, and a booking can even span two subsequent plans when their slot configuration allows it.

To book capacity, you use the **Slot Picker** page, where you can filter by satellite and transponder to select an available slot. This page is integrated with the [MediaOps Plan](xref:MediaOps.Plan) Scheduling app, so bookings stay aligned with the rest of your planning. For more details on that integration, see [Link with scheduling app](xref:SS_Link_With_Scheduling_App).
