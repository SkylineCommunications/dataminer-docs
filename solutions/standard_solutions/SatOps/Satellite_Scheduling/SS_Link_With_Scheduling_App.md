# Link with scheduling app

The Satellite Scheduling app is tightly integrated with the [MediaOps Plan](xref:MediaOps.Plan) Scheduling app. Transponder slots defined in the [Satellite Inventory](xref:Satellite_Inventory) app are exposed as bookable capacity, and the **Slot Picker** page lets you select an available slot (filtered by satellite and transponder) directly from within the scheduling workflow.

Because transponder resources are kept in sync with MediaOps Plan Resource Studio, bookings made for satellite capacity stay aligned with the rest of your planning in MediaOps.
