# Managing transponders

A *transponder* belongs to a [beam](xref:SI_Managing_Beams) and has a bandwidth that is divided into slots. In the Satellite Inventory app, you manage transponders together with their plans and slots.

Using the app, you can:

- Create, edit, and manage transponders through interactive dialogs.
- Define time-boxed *transponder plans* with configurable slot sizes, and configure a permanent base plan.
- Generate *transponder slots* from a plan and inspect individual slots from a dedicated panel.
- Visualize transponder plan layouts using a CSS grid representation.
- Navigate directly to the corresponding view in the [Satellite Scheduling](xref:Satellite_Scheduling) app.
- Keep transponder resources in sync with [MediaOps Plan](xref:MediaOps.Plan) Resource Studio, including automatic activation and deprecation.
